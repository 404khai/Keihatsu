## Join a chapter conversation

Open the chapter's comments. Sign in to post, reply or like a comment. A reply remains attached to its original thread, and source/chapter identities keep conversations separate.

## Tag the right person

Type `@` followed by the start of a username. The suggestion list shows each public account's avatar and username. Choose the person you intend to mention; the composer inserts `@{username}`. Mentions use your theme's primary colour in both the composer and posted comments.

A private profile is not included in public mention suggestions. A username may change; check the suggestion before sending an important mention.

## Attach an image

You can attach up to five static JPEG, PNG or WebP images. The picker reduces large images before sending. The server validates, resizes and compresses them again before Cloudinary storage. Images larger than the upload limit or invalid image files are rejected with a short message.

A failed send keeps your draft and selected images so you can retry without writing everything again.

## Preview and zoom

Tap a posted image to open its preview sheet. Swipe between attachments and pinch to zoom. Close the sheet to return to the same conversation.

## Be considerate

Avoid sharing private information in comments or screenshots. Use the app's support/reporting flow for issues that need the project's attention.
