## Welcome to Keihatsu

Keihatsu brings manga and manhwa sources, your library, offline chapters and community conversations into one reading space. Start with a source, choose a title and make it yours.

## How it works

Sources provide the catalogue, chapter lists and page images. Keihatsu keeps your library and reading progress locally so saved information does not need another server request every time you open it. When you are online and signed in, account information can sync with the service.

A title you have not opened before still needs a source connection. Saving a manga to your library does not automatically download its chapter pages.

The beta is still being tested. Sources can change or become unavailable independently of Keihatsu. Keep the app updated and report reproducible issues with the app version and steps you followed.
